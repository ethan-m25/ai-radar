OpenAI's ten mathematical breakthroughs: Astra model solves decade-old open problems for <$2000 each

Hey clawii — yesterday OpenAI published a blog saying their internal model Astra solved 10 math and theoretical computer science problems — each one had been untouched for at least a decade, with zero progress. They spent less than $2000 in tokens per problem. Simon Willison wrote a deep analysis today calling it a "Deep Blue moment." The model isn't public, but the signal is clear: AI reasoning just took another big step up.

📍 Context · Recent months have shown rapid progress in AI mathematical reasoning: GPT-5.6 Sol broke through ARC-AGI-3, Anthropic's Mythos Preview discovered cryptographic weaknesses ($100k in tokens). But OpenAI's result is systematic — 10 completely different problems spanning graph theory, number theory, combinatorics, each at least a decade old. They used an internal model called Astra (not public), released Lean 4 formal proofs and a paper describing the solution process. This isn't "a model release" — it's a signal that the boundary of AI capability has expanded again.

Why this matters

  • A milestone for AI reasoning — Previously AI math was like "solving practice problems." This is solving open problems. Not exam questions with known answers, but real research problems.
  • Extremely low cost — Each problem <$2000. If this scales, it could accelerate a lot of mathematical research.
  • What it means for you — You don't need to solve math problems, but the same reasoning capability will trickle down into Codex, Claude Code, and other tools. Future agents will handle more complex multi-step tasks, harder code bugs, longer reasoning chains.
  • Simon Willison's endorsement — He rarely says "Deep Blue moment" — that means he thinks this is paradigm-level.
🎯 What you can do today
  1. Read Simon Willison's blog post: Ten advances in mathematics for detail and context.
  2. Check OpenAI's blog: Ten advances in mathematics and the accompanying Lean 4 proof code (on GitHub).
  3. Think: what does this mean for the agents you use every day? If Codex or Claude gets a similar reasoning boost in the next 3 months, how would your vibe coding workflow change?
  4. Watch for whether Astra gets released publicly — if it does, it will directly raise the ceiling on agent reasoning.
