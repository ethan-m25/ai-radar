👀 Worth a look

Gemini sends Drive files to human review

A developer dug up that Gemini will hand your personal Drive files and emails to humans to review. If your company uses Google Workspace, be careful with payroll data.

Why it matters

  • There's a repo on GitHub with the evidence, HN 633 points
  • Sensitive files like payroll and performance reviews could be seen by third parties
  • Worth checking the data terms before your company uses Gemini

🎯 What you can do

  1. Ask your company IT/security one question: will Gemini data be reviewed by humans
  2. Keep sensitive payroll files out of Drive folders that auto-sync
🔬 Deep dive (tap to open)

This isn't a bug, it's Google's data handling being exposed. For your comp work at a multinational, it's a compliance point worth flagging in advance.

Whether it's real and how wide it goes depends on your company's contract terms, don't just trust a post; but asking a question doesn't hurt.
