Gemini launches a decision model API

Google just dropped a "decision model" (an AI built for yes/no calls and scoring), and it can look at videos and PDFs directly. It's so cheap it's basically free, perfect for dropping into your little tools.

Why it matters

  • Lets AI give docs/videos a yes/no or a score, and it's more reliable than chat models
  • It can judge text, images, audio, video, and PDFs
  • It joins similar models from Jev and OpenAI to make "decision models" a new category

🎯 What you can do

  1. Read Simon Willison's hands-on test to see how it judges video content (simonwillison.net/tags/ai)
  2. Grab one of your files and try a yes/no question on a small sample first to check accuracy
🔬 Deep dive (tap to open)

Unlike the Claude/codex you use: it doesn't chat, it quickly scores and sorts fixed questions.

Text and images put it in the same league as OpenAI's; multimodal (video/audio/PDF) is Gemini's unique trick.

Low barrier to entry, but you have to go through the API, so you'll need an agent to write a few lines of code to call it. Cheap doesn't mean accurate, so validate on a small sample first.

👀 Worth a look

Yogurt: open-source meeting notes

An open-source alternative to Granola (the AI meeting notes app), free and focused on privacy. If you want to take meeting notes without uploading recordings to the cloud, take a look.

Why it matters

  • A free, open-source meeting notes AI, one more option to have
  • Privacy-first, good for sensitive stuff like salary talks
  • Self-hostable, so your data doesn't have to go to the cloud

🎯 What you can do

  1. Check the install steps on GitHub (github.com/frigadehq)
  2. Try it on one internal meeting first, don't jump straight to sensitive stuff
🔬 Deep dive (tap to open)

Unlike the cloud notes you use now: open source, self-hostable, and you get more control over privacy.

It's just an alternative to Granola, and it's an early project, so you'll have to test the transcription quality yourself.

Setup is medium-effort, you might need Docker or local config, and it's not click-and-go.
