284B DeepSeek squeezed into 64G of RAM

Someone open-sourced a project that gets the 284B DeepSeek running on a 64G machine with no GPU. Your Mac mini might be able to run a big model locally too.

Why it matters

  • No GPU needed, which saves on GPU costs and dodges privacy worries
  • 432 points and 193 comments on HN, the community is testing it out
  • Local means your data never leaves the machine, good for sensitive spreadsheets

🎯 What you can do

  1. Check the repo README: https://github.com/synchopate/llama-modchip
  2. Try a small model on your Mac first, then decide if you want to wrestle with the 284B
🔬 Deep dive (tap to open)

Unlike the cloud Claude/Codex you use now, this puts the weights on your own machine, so it runs even offline.

The tradeoff is it'll probably be really slow (a few characters per second), and 284B is a tight fit for 64G of RAM, so it's mostly leaning on SSD swap.

Non-developers can try it, but don't expect it to feel as smooth as Opus 5.5.
