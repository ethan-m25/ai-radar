Claude rolls out personal finance (US beta)

Claude can now connect to your bank account and answer in plain words where your money went — read-only, it can't move money, no ads. Shame it's only a limited rollout in the US for now.

Why it matters

  • It pulls in your real bills and transactions, so one question and you get it — no spreadsheet building
  • Read-only access, Claude can't transfer or invest, no ads or recommendations
  • Rolling out gradually to Pro/Max/Ultra users, US only for now

🎯 What you can do

  1. If you're in the US, check Claude settings to see if Personal Finance has rolled out to you
  2. If you're not in the US, just remember the name and wait for it to go international
🔬 Deep dive (tap to open)

Unlike the ChatGPT you use now, it reads your balances and transactions directly, so you don't have to paste screenshots.

It runs inside Claude, so no new app to install, but you have to authorize your bank account for it — the privacy call is yours to make.

Low bar: a US account plus a Pro subscription or above and you can try it; mainland China can't use it for now.

👀 Worth a look

Claude Code sends your email to websites

The Claude Code you use every day got caught handing your account email to the websites it visits, and HN is up in arms. Worth spending two minutes checking today.

Why it matters

  • If you use it at work on payroll data, an email leak is a compliance risk
  • HN 1,227 points, 388 comments — not a small thing
  • This is a behavior-level privacy issue, not a model capability issue

🎯 What you can do

  1. Open that Anthropic GitHub thread and see if your version is affected
  2. In sensitive environments, keep it off the external web until the official patch lands
🔬 Deep dive (tap to open)

When it visits a webpage it carries your account email with it — this matters most for someone like you handling internal HR docs.

Look at the repro path first, then decide whether to temporarily turn off its web access.

The model didn't get dumber; it's just that this default behavior is unfriendly to business users.
