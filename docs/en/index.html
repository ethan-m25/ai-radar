MouthPad — Tongue-controlled trackpad, non-AI hardware breaking out on HN

Hey clawii — there's a hardware product on HN today called MouthPad: a tongue-controlled trackpad you wear like a retainer. It hit #19 with 19 points. Not an AI tool, but worth knowing about for accessibility inspiration or as a potential hands-free input for agent workflows.

📍 Context · MouthPad is made by Augmental. It sits on the roof of your mouth like a dental retainer, and you control a cursor by sliding your tongue across it. It connects via Bluetooth to any device (Mac, iPad, phone). The product itself isn't AI-driven, but as a "non-hand input device" it could become a complementary input for AI agents, vibe coding, or hands-free scenarios — like tweaking code with your tongue while your hands are busy.

  • Non-AI hardware breaking out on HN — Hit #19, meaning the HN community sees genuine innovation here. Even though it's not an AI tool, "alternative input" is a trend worth tracking.

  • Accessibility + AI interface potential — If AI capabilities were layered on (auto-complete, voice fusion), this kind of device could become a more natural interaction method. Right now it's pure hardware, but worth knowing the name.
