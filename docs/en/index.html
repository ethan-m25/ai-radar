👀 Worth a look

Gemini 4 Neon is out

Google launched Gemini 4 Neon. It's pitched as fast, cheap, and good at following tricky instructions. People on HN are talking about it a lot.

Why it matters

  • Josh Woodward says it follows instructions well and is faster and cheaper
  • HN gave it 867 points and 686 comments, so lots of people are watching
  • Simon Willison tested it: its image drawing beats Hydrogen but loses to Carbon

🎯 What you can do

  1. Switch to Neon in the Gemini app and try it on a pay analysis task you do often
  2. Run the same task on Claude and Gemini and compare the results
🔬 Deep dive (tap to open)

Gemini 4 now comes in three tiers: Carbon, Neon, and Hydrogen. Neon sits in the middle, a trade-off between speed and cost.

The pricing is a mess: Neon costs $3 per million input tokens and $15 for output. Carbon is half price right now, at $2/$10, but goes up to $4/$20 starting December 1.

Simon says Neon makes the pricing even more confusing, so don't just go by price. Try it yourself first.
