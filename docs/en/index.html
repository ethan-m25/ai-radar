Claude connectors and skills are now one thing: "plugins"

What it is

Before, hooking Claude up to outside tools (connectors, like Gmail or Drive) and teaching it new tricks (skills, a set of written instructions for how to do a task) were managed separately. Now they're merged into "plugins": a plugin can just connect tools, just teach skills, or do both. claude.ai/plugins is the one place to browse, and the official count is 10,000+.

What it means

You don't have to hop between a few different pages to find tools and add skills anymore, since it's all in one place. It doesn't change much for you, but it'll make it easier to connect Claude to the software your company uses.

🎯 How to use it

  1. Log in to claude.ai, look for "Plugins" in the sidebar (it used to be under Customize), then browse and install.
  2. It's available on the Free, Pro, Max and Ultra plans. Officially, Team and Enterprise are coming soon.
🔬 Deep dive (tap to open)

According to the official support docs: deleting a plugin doesn't automatically disconnect the connectors it came with. You have to disconnect them yourself, or the permissions just stay there.

Public plugins come in two kinds: "Verified" and "Community". Before installing a community plugin, check what data of yours it wants to connect to.

Plugins you install through the Claude Code command line stay on that one computer. They don't sync to your claude.ai account.

ChatGPT Dots: an agent that acts without you asking

What it is

Dots is an agent in ChatGPT (an AI helper that can go do things on its own). According to OpenAI, it also does "proactive research" in the background while you're away, using only read-only tools to look at the apps you've connected. It can also check in on a schedule, and it can get things done through Slack, Teams and 4,000+ app plugins.

What it means

AI is going from "you ask, it answers" to "it keeps an eye on things for you". It's not reliable enough yet, so keep a close watch on anything like sending emails.

🎯 How to use it

  1. Create a new dot in the ChatGPT desktop app or in a desktop browser. Reportedly, at launch it's for ChatGPT Pro and Business Premium, and Enterprise needs an admin to turn on the beta. Check the prompts inside ChatGPT to see if your plan has it.
🔬 Deep dive (tap to open)

OpenAI says the background research is read-only, and you can also set "custom rules" so certain actions need approval or are blocked.

User Peter Yang reported that Dots sometimes sent emails without him reviewing them, and also suddenly stopped responding. OpenAI itself says dots can make mistakes, so double-check anything important.

Unlike the Claude Code and codex you use, this one is mainly for everyday tasks, not a tool built specifically for writing code.

ℹ️ FYI

Claude Ship: an enterprise tool that lets AI handle PRs

What it is

Claude Ship (beta) is a feature Anthropic built for Team and Enterprise customers to manage pull requests (PRs, which are the "please review and merge my changes" requests programmers submit). Officially, Claude can review PRs, fix issues itself, and approve the PRs that team rules allow. The goal is to get merging code up to the speed of writing it. I couldn't find any more detailed official info.

What it means

AI already writes code fast, and now review and merging are being handed to AI too. You won't use it personally unless the dev team at your company adopts it.
