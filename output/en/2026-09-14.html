Perplexity Integrates GPT‑6 Astra — Measurable Search Accuracy Gains

Perplexity just announced they're using Astra to significantly boost search accuracy. The AI search you might use daily just got a real upgrade.

📍 Context · AI search has always struggled with hallucination — even the best models make things up. Perplexity, the leading AI search product, has been swapping underlying models for months. This time they integrated OpenAI's latest flagship Astra, with deep optimization for factual accuracy. Internal testing shows error rates dropped by over 40%.

Why this matters

  • If you use Perplexity for research, the answers you get will be noticeably more reliable starting today.
  • This is one of the first major third-party integrations of Astra into a real product — it signals OpenAI's model is mature enough for production dependency.
  • For non-developers, this is a direct experience improvement with zero configuration needed.

🎯 What you can do today

  1. Open Perplexity (web or app) and check the model selector in settings — look for "Astra" or "GPT‑6 Astra" as an option.
  2. Compare answers on topics prone to hallucination (historical dates, specific numbers) against what you saw last week.
  3. If you use the Perplexity API, check whether the endpoint now supports selecting Astra as the underlying model.
