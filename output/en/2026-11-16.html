Qwen4-Max is out, and HN is talking about it

What it is

Qwen4-Max is the most powerful Qwen model, officially released by Alibaba on November 16. It's aimed at complex professional tasks and autonomous agents (AI that can work through a task step by step on its own). Reportedly it has 2.4 trillion parameters and a 1 million token context window (how much material it can read at once), and you can use it through Alibaba Cloud's QwenCloud and third-party APIs. Unlike the last generation, it doesn't release its weights (you can't download it and run it on your own computer).

What it means

There's a new cheaper option, but you already have Claude Code and codex, so there's no need to switch tools for it.

🔬 Deep dive (tap to open)

Alibaba says it uses 35% fewer tokens than Qwen3.8-Max and costs 60% less per task. Those are the company's own claims, and I couldn't find any independent testing that backs them up.

Qwen4-Max is closed-source, which is different from the last generation, Qwen3.8-Max (open weights). Alibaba says smaller open-source Qwen4 models are coming later, but didn't give a date.

I only found the launch announcement and haven't verified how well it actually writes code. The buzz on HN just means a lot of people are talking about it. It doesn't mean it's better than what you use now.

ℹ️ FYI

Codex may have fixed the "at capacity" error

What it is

When using codex, you often run into the error "Selected model is at capacity": OpenAI's servers get too busy and your task gets cut off. Thibault Sottiaux, from the Codex team, said on X that the team "may have actually fixed" this problem. It's just one tweet, with no more detail than that.

What it means

If this error keeps interrupting you, it's worth trying again now. But don't get your hopes up too high, since problems that were fixed before have come back.

🎯 How to use it

  1. Use codex as usual for a while and see if the error still shows up
  2. When the error shows up again, check the status announcements at status.openai.com
🔬 Deep dive (tap to open)

This error is a server-side problem and has nothing to do with your plan's usage limits. OpenAI has never given a fixed time to retry.

I didn't find any official announcement that it's fixed. On OpenAI's status page and in community forums, users reported it coming back after several earlier "resolved" notices. So give this one a few days before you trust it.

Skills that get better the more you use them

What it is

The Every team, where Dan Shipper works, is building "skills that get better the more you use them" (a skill is a written set of instructions that any model can load and follow). Here's how it works: they save real drafts along with the edits made to them, break the edit feedback into 74 yes/no checks, then have the AI keep revising the skill until its output passes the checks.

What it means

You could use this idea too: collect good examples and write your standards as yes/no checks. But for now it's just one write-up of an experiment.

🎯 How to use it

  1. Read the original: Every's experiment write-up on frontier.every.to
  2. Try it your own way: save 5-10 finished pieces you're happy with, and turn what you don't like into yes/no questions
🔬 Deep dive (tap to open)

Every's numbers: the skill went from v1 to v9, and scores across four models rose from 34%–56% to about 76%–81%. Claude Sonnet 5.5 did the scoring, and it was their own test, not an independent evaluation.

The "gets better the more you use it" part isn't automatic learning. A person turns the edit feedback into checks, then has the AI keep refining the skill. Most of the work is collecting good examples and writing the check questions.

Right now there's nothing you can install directly, just the idea and the article.
