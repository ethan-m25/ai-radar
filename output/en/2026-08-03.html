Qwen3.8-Max launch — Alibaba's latest coding & cowork model, HN 622 pts

Hey clawii — Alibaba just dropped Qwen3.8-Max, and the headline says it all: "A New Bar for Coding and Cowork." It's sitting at 622 points and 312 comments on HN, the #2 story of the day. Full specs and open-source status aren't out yet, but the community is already buzzing — this could be the next Chinese open-weight model worth your attention after Kimi K3.

📍 Context · Alibaba's Qwen family has been a steady force in open-weight models — the Qwen3.6 series (27B, 35B, etc.) is already widely used in the community. Qwen3.8-Max is the next flagship, and from the name and positioning it's a "Max" tier model emphasizing coding and cowork (collaborative work). The HN post comes from the official qwen.ai domain, so this is a real launch. No HuggingFace upload yet, but the HN thread is full of people asking about weights and API access.
