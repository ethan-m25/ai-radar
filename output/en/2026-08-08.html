🚨 MUST SEE

DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731 — open weights, near-zero cost, suddenly blowing up on HN

clawii, if you want to save money on agents without giving up too much quality, DeepSeek V4 Flash might be your workhorse model next month. It took #3 on Hacker News today with 646 points and 393 comments — the community suddenly noticed its ARC-AGI scores are closing in on GPT-5.6 Luna while costing ~60% less per task. Weights are fully open (MIT), and you can run it locally.

📍 Background The open-weights race is white-hot. Chinese labs (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) keep pushing prices down while lifting reasoning ability. DeepSeek V4 Flash shipped its 0731 build on July 31, but buzz was modest at the time. Today a Hacker News post linking to arcprize.org showed its ARC-AGI performance, and stacked on top of the cost advantage that had been building, the community suddenly lit up. HN #3, 646 points, 393 comments.

Why this matters

  • ~60% of Luna's cost per task, while scoring 82.7 on Terminal-Bench vs Luna's 51. For someone running agent tasks all day, your monthly bill could roughly halve.
  • Open weights + MIT license: deploy locally, quantize (4-bit or 3-bit both work), or use Ollama Cloud. No data leaving your control, no sudden API price hikes.
  • 1M context + 284B total / 13B active MoE. Long-horizon work (whole-repo refactors, self-built agents) is where this architecture shines.
🎯 Things you can do today
  1. Open the HuggingFace model card. 786k downloads, strong community support.
  2. Run a test prompt on Ollama Cloud and compare it head-to-head with your Claude Code / Codex tasks.
  3. If you use Codex or Claude Code, point the base model at DeepSeek V4 Flash in config (OpenAI-compatible API). Try it on a low-stakes task first.

What it runs on: MoE architecture, 284B params (13B active). GGUF quantized versions on HuggingFace (by unsloth). vLLM and Ollama both natively support it.

How hard to use / build: API-only is a 5-minute job. Self-hosting needs at least one 48GB VRAM GPU (e.g., A6000) for the 4-bit build. Community tutorials are thorough.

Honest take: It's a beast on coding-agent evals, but general knowledge / creative writing trails Luna or Opus 5. If you mostly generate code and run agents, it's the cost-performance king. For long-form writing or deep reasoning, stay on Luna.

Jargon decoder
ARC-AGI = Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus for Artificial General Intelligence — a visual-puzzle benchmark for reasoning. High scores mean better abstraction and generalization, not just memorization.
MoE = Mixture of Experts — splits a huge model into many small experts and activates only a subset per token, keeping capability while slashing compute cost.
