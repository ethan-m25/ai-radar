HackerBooks: the books HN keeps recommending

Same trick, different target: dig up the books that keep coming up across 20 years of HN threads and turn them into a site. Free, no login—perfect for a weekend browse.

Why it matters

  • The list comes from real comments, not a ranking gamed by votes
  • Wikihack and HackerHack popped up in the same wave
  • Opens on mobile, no signup, zero barrier

🎯 What you can do

  1. Open hackerbooks.org and poke around
  2. If you want to find stuff by wiki entry, try wikihack.netlify.app
🔬 Deep dive (tap to open)

Unlike Douban or Goodreads, it doesn't give ratings—it just counts the books that keep getting mentioned in HN comments.

Pure web page, static index, loads fast; basically turns 'what HN has mentioned' into a searchable catalog.

Limitation: it skews toward the English tech crowd; new books and Chinese books are basically missing, so don't expect it to work as a mainstream book list.
